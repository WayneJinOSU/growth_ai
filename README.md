### —— The "Blue Sky" Edition
**"Full-Dimensional Strategic" Combat System for Mid-to-Large Cap Growth Stocks**

> **Status**: LIVE (Strategic Integration)
> **Core Evolution**: Evolved from "finding growth" to "finding **undervalued explosive power**."
> **Core Philosophy**:
> *   **Quality**: Moat determines how long you survive.
> *   **Blue Sky**: The second curve determines how high you fly.
> *   **Waves**: Industry-wide demand determines how fast you take off.

## 🚫 Phase 0: Gatekeeper
**Goal**: Filter out 90% of the noise using the "Elimination Method."

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Ticker Input"] --> B["Blacklist Check"]
    B -- "Fashion/Regional Banks" --> Fail["❌ SKIP"]
    B -- "Clear" --> G["Future Growth Check"]
    G -- "CAGR < 20%" --> Fail
    G -- "Pass" --> M["Macro Mode Check"]
    M -- "VIX > 30 / Yield Surge" --> Fail
    M -- "Safe" --> Out["✅ Valid Candidate"]
```

1.  **🚫 The Kill Zone**: Fashion, regional banks, asset-heavy/low-margin businesses.
2.  **⚡️ The Iron Rule**: Expected revenue CAGR > 20% for the next 3 years.
3.  **📉 Macro Pressure Valve**: Triggers circuit breaker if VIX > 30 or Treasury yields surge.

---

## 🔍 Phase 1: Deep Audit
**Goal**: Tiered "physical exam" of business models to identify true revenue quality.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Candidate"] --> Seg["Business Model Identifier"]
    Seg -- "SaaS" --> SaaS["NDR/RPO Audit"]
    Seg -- "Hard Tech" --> Hard["Inventory/B2B Audit"]
    Seg -- "Consumables" --> Cons["Rule of 40 Audit"]
    SaaS & Hard & Cons --> Lie["Lie Detector Check"]
    Lie -- "CFO-Divergence / Insider Selling" --> Fail["❌ FAIL"]
    Lie -- "Clean" --> Out["✅ Hygiene Passed"]
```

*   **SaaS**: NDR > 110%, RPO growth > Revenue growth.
*   **Hard Tech**: Book-to-Bill > 1.0, strictly prevent "Inventory Death Cross."
*   **Platform/Consumables**: Rule of 40, LTV/CAC > 3.
*   **Lie Detector**: Audit CFO/Net Income divergence and assess insider selling risks.

---

## 🕵️ Phase 2: Shadow Audit
**Goal**: Verify business status through external evidence.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Hygiene Passed"] --> King["King-Maker Audit"]
    King -- "Apple/Microsoft Customers" --> Trust["Strong Endorsement"]
    King -- "None" --> Peer["Peer Review"]
    Trust & Peer --> Org["Organic Growth Validation"]
    Org -- "S&M Efficiency Check" --> Sniper["🎯 Sniper Score"]
    Org --> Sand["Sandbagging Detection"]
```

*   **King Makers**: Are giants like Apple, Microsoft, or Nvidia among the customers?
*   **Organic Growth**: S&M expenses decreasing while revenue per customer increases.
*   **Sandbagging Detection**: Does management have a habit of providing conservative guidance but consistently beating expectations?

---

## 🧠 Phase 3: Intelligence
**Goal**: Qualitative analysis of management integrity, moat dynamics, and KPI verification.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Audit Verified"] --> Search["Real-time KPI Search"]
    Search --> Compare["Wall Street Consensus vs Reality"]
    Compare --> Integrity["Management Integrity Assessment"]
    Integrity -- "Over-promising" --> Alert["⚠️ Alert"]
    Integrity -- "Sandbagging" --> Bonus["✨ Bonus Points"]
```

*   **KPI Search Verification**: Real-time search and comparison of industry-specific metrics (e.g., ARR, DAU).
*   **Moat Assessment**: Is the competitive advantage expanding or eroding?

## 🔭 Phase 4: Blue Sky Analysis
**Goal**: Identify "premium engines" driving high valuations.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Intel Gathered"] --> TAM["TAM Explosion Check"]
    TAM -- "New Boundary Found" --> RND["Second Curve Audit"]
    RND -- "Growth > 50% & Rev > 10%" --> Trigger["🚀 BLUE SKY TRIGGERED"]
    Trigger --> Rerate["Relax PEG Limit to 2.5"]
```

*   **TAM Explosion**: Logic of doubling Total Addressable Market due to technological breakthroughs.
*   **Second Growth Curve**: New business growth > 50% and accounting for > 10% of revenue.
*   **Valuation Exemption**: For companies triggering "Blue Sky" logic, the PEG limit can be relaxed to 2.5.

## 🌊 Phase 5: Catalysts & Waves
**Core Principle: Macro Need > Micro Events**

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["Growth Logic"] --> Wave["Thematic Wave Check"]
    Wave -- "Inverting Macro Need" --> Primary["🔥 Primary Catalyst"]
    Primary --> Hard["Hard Event Tracking"]
    Hard -- "Earnings/Product Cycles" --> Secondary["📅 Secondary Catalyst"]
```

*   **Primary Catalysts (Thematic Waves)**: Irreversible industry momentum (e.g., labor shortages driving AI demand).
*   **Secondary Catalysts (Hard Events)**: Specific validation nodes like earnings reports, product launches, or investor days.

## ♟️ Phase 6: Strategic Pricing
**Goal**: Convert all qualitative intelligence into quantitative operational instructions.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    In["All Signals"] --> VScrub["Valuation Scrub"]
    VScrub --> Fortress["Fortress Test: Tier 1/2/3"]
    Fortress --> Matrix["The Executive Matrix"]
    Matrix -- "High Catalyst & Reasonable Val" --> Diamond["💎 Diamond Setup"]
    Matrix -- "Low Catalyst & Tier 1" --> Acc["🏰 Accumulate"]
```

1.  **Valuation Scrub**: Adjust valuation multiples based on management integrity (Sandbagger/Over-Promiser).
2.  **Fortress Test**: Certification for core holdings, categorized into Tier 1/2/3.
3.  **Executive Matrix**: Combines catalyst strength and valuation status to output:
    *   💎 **Diamond Setup** (The perfect entry point)
    *   🚀 **Momentum Ride** (Trend-driven chasing)
    *   🏰 **Fortress Accumulation** (Building base positions on the left side)

---

## 🚀 Phase 7: Physics VPA
**Goal**: Institutional behavior analysis based on the SMA20 lifeline.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Price["SMA20 Timeline"] --> Ignite["Price > SMA20 + RVol > 2.0"]
    Ignite --> FIRE["🚀 IGNITION"]
    Price --> Accum["Range < 2% + RVol > 1.5"]
    Accum --> ACC["🔋 ACCUMULATION"]
    Price --> Broken["Close < SMA20 for 3 Days"]
    Broken --> EJECT["📉 BROKEN TREND"]
```

*   **Ignition**: High-volume breakout of SMA20, RVol > 2.0, strong close.
*   **Accumulation**: Tight range (< 2%) + volume (RVol > 1.5).
*   **Broken Trend**: Closing below SMA20 for 3 consecutive days.

## 📑 Citation System
The system implements **academic-grade citation tagging** in reports:
- **In-text Citations**: Real-time annotation of data sources using `[1]`, `[2]`, etc.
- **Dynamic Indexing**: Automatically tracks search sources and generates a `References & Sources` list at the footer.
- **Anti-Hallucination**: ID indexing ensures the LLM retrieves real search results and links.
